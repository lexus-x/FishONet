The quota is a trade, not a free lunch.

Move the routing threshold f and watch seen accuracy trade against novel recall. This is why f = 0.60, not a rounder number, is what shipped.

0.40, aggressive novel 0.60, deployed 0.80, closed-set bias

A correctly retained seen image is worth about 54% of the score, a novel image about 23%. Push f too low and seen accuracy pays for it. Push it too high and the novel head starves.

Our best validation gain was an artifact.

An early re-ranker showed +13.374 on the validation split and +0.006 on the real leaderboard. Here is the post-mortem.

Leaky validation pool

+13.374 proxy, +0.006 real

The candidate pool held 1,159 pseudo-novel classes among 11,598 ineligible distractors. The model learned to spot the 9.1% subpopulation, not true morphology.

Leak-free pool

+8.154 proxy, +0.213 real

Restricting the pool to gold-eligible classes with within-query normalization dropped the proxy gain, and multiplied the real leaderboard gain thirty-five times over.

What did not work sets the real ceiling.

Knowing which levers saturated is how you find the actual bound on open-set zero-shot recognition.
