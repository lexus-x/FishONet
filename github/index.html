The gate is a quota, not a threshold.

All 35,665 images are ranked by novelty. The top 60% go to the seen head — a fixed count, so only the ordering matters and the gate's probabilities are discarded entirely.

The finding we are proudest of

Our best-ever result was the model cheating.

A re-ranker scored +13.374 on our validation set and +0.006 on the real leaderboard. A gap that size is not optimism — it means the model found something in the test harness that does not exist in reality.

Before — the pool had a tell

9.1%

Only 1,159 of 12,757 candidates could ever be the answer — and that subgroup is detectable from the features. The model learned "prefer the eligible-looking ones", picking them 53.7% of the time against the fusion's 38.4%.

After — every candidate is eligible

100%

Restrict the pool so the shortcut carries no information. The validation number fell to +8.154 — and the real score rose to +0.213, a 35× improvement in what actually counts.
